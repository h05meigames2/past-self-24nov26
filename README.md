A two-line warning to my past self about one specific moment — a text sent at 2am from the kitchen table on the night she said she was leaving — and one sentence on why that moment mattered and what changed because of it. The program validates the two lines (exactly two lines, one sentence each, 1–280 characters) and writes them to `/output/entry.txt`.

Entry for the [Two Lines to Your Past Self bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This prints the two lines and writes them to `/output/entry.txt` (falling back to `./output/entry.txt` if `/output` is not writable), then verifies the file round-trips byte for byte.
